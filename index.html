For larger, centered equations, we can use standard double-dollar delimiters:

$$ex(n, C_4) = \frac{1}{2}n^{3/2} + o(n^{3/2})$$

When $q$ is a prime power and $n = q^2 + q + 1$, the unique extremal graphs are derived from projective planes.
